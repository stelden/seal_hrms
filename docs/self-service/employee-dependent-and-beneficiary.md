**An employee dependent and beneficiary is a master record for one person connected to a member of staff, as a dependant, a beneficiary, or both.**

Peter Otieno records his daughter as a dependant and his wife as a dependant and beneficiary.

**Where:** My HR → search bar → **Employee Dependent and Beneficiary** (it has no sidebar entry)

## Before you start

Only a **System Manager** can open the list unless your site gives other roles access.

## Create an employee dependent and beneficiary

1. Open the **Employee Dependent and Beneficiary** list and click **+ Add Employee Dependent and Beneficiary**.
2. Choose the **Employee**.
3. Enter the **First Name** as on their ID.
4. Choose the **Gender**.
5. Choose the **Type**: **Dependent**, **Beneficiary**, or **Dependent and Beneficiary**.
6. Choose the **Relationship**, such as **Spouse** or **Parent**.
7. Click **Save**.

## Fields worth knowing

| Field | What it does |
|:---|:---|
| **Middle Name**, **Last Name** | As on their ID. |
| **Date of Birth** | Used to work out **Age**, and whether a child is still eligible. |
| **Contact Details** | How to reach them if the member of staff cannot be reached. |
