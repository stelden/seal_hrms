**Where your site has My Desk, everyone with people reporting to them gets a Line Manager page: what waits for their decision, who in their team is away this week, and quick links to their team's records.**

## Why it matters

A manager's HR work is spread across leave, expense claims, timesheets and time off. The Line Manager page puts it in one place, on the desk and on the phone, without anyone setting it up person by person.

## How it works

| Part | What it shows |
|:---|:---|
| **Approvals** | Everything waiting for your decision, HR's included: leave, expense claims, time off claims and timesheets, and those of anyone whose approvals you give while they are away. |
| **Who's Away This Week** | Leave this week (Monday to Sunday), approved or waiting, of the people who report to you and of the people who report to them. Only leave you are allowed to open is listed; tap a line to open it. |
| **Cover** | Who covers your work, whose work you cover, and whose approvals you give. |
| **Timesheets Waiting for You**, **Time Off in Lieu Waiting for You** | Timesheets and time off claims to decide or put right. |
| **My Tasks** | Your open tasks. |
| Menu **Team** | **My Team** (the people who report to you), leave applications, expense claims, timesheets, time off claims and handovers. |

**Who gets it.** Everyone whose employee record has at least one active employee reporting to them (**Reports To**) and who signs in to the desk. They are given the **Line Manager** role automatically: when an employee record changes, and once a day. When nobody reports to them any more, the role is taken away again, unless someone gave it by hand or through a role profile.

**Setting it up.** The page arrives as a draft offered to the Line Manager role. A Desk Manager opens **Desk Page** "Line Manager", checks who gets it, changes the priority or what people may change if needed, and publishes it. To change its parts, duplicate it.

## Where you see it

My Desk, on the desk (the page switcher) and on the phone (Home). See also [SEAL HRMS on My Desk](my-desk.md).
