**You will have agreed to cover a colleague's work, covered it while they were away, and approved in their place where asked.**

**Who:** the named stand-in or acting approver · **Before you start:** a colleague has named you on a [Task Assignment](task-assignment.md) and you have been emailed.

## Steps

1. Open the handover from the email, or from **Handovers waiting for you** on My Desk.
2. Read the **Handover Notes** and the **Work** tab.
3. Click **Accept** if you can cover it, or **Decline** and say why.

Your colleague reads your reason to decide who to ask instead. If you are asked to approve on their behalf, answer each approval on its own: under the **Approvals** menu, click **Accept: ...** or **Decline: ...** for each one. Agreeing to cover someone's work is not agreeing to sign for them.

Once everyone has answered, your colleague is emailed. Until the leave starts you can still click **Decline** if you can no longer cover it.

## What happens next

### While you are covering

When the leave is approved and has started, the work moves to your to-do list and you are emailed. The handover's status is **Active**.

- Click **Leave a Note**, choose the task and say what you did, so your colleague can pick up where you left off.
- Before they are back, click **Note for Return**: what you finished, what is waiting, and who called. You are reminded the day before their return date.

On their return date, anything still open goes back to them. What you finished stays finished. A task you already had before the handover stays with you.

### Approve in someone's place

If you accepted an approval on a handover, then while the handover is **Active**:

- you are given the approver role you need, if you did not already have it;
- the documents waiting for your colleague's approval are shared with you, and so are new ones raised while they are away (checked every hour);
- each approval you give records on the document that you approved for them during their leave.

You cannot approve your own request, even though your own approver is the person you are standing in for. Someone else must approve it.

When your colleague returns, the role and the shared documents are taken back. A role you already held is left alone.

The approvals a handover can pass on include leave, expense claims, time off in lieu and timesheets. A head of department can hand over everything they approve for their department in one line, **Approving for my department as Head of Department**.

:::note
If you go on leave yourself while covering, the approvals do not pass on to your own stand-in.
:::

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Do this |
|:---|:---|
| "Only the person named to approve this can answer for it." | Answer only the approvals that name you. |
| "Only whoever covers this task can leave notes on it." | Leave notes only on tasks you cover. |
| "This is your own timesheet. Someone else must approve it." / "This is your own claim. Someone else must decide it." | Ask another approver or HR. |
