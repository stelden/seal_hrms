**Who is covering for colleagues in a period, and is anyone covering too much?**

**Where:** My HR → Self Service → Setup → Stand-in Load · **Who can run it:** HR User, HR Manager, System Manager.

## Filters

| Filter | Use it to |
|:---|:---|
| **Company** | Only this company's staff. |
| **From**, **To** | The period (required). |

## Columns

| Column | Shows |
|:---|:---|
| **Stand-in**, **Name** | The person covering. |
| **People Covered**, **Covering For** | How many colleagues, and who. |
| **Tasks**, **Approvals** | What they cover. |
| **At Once** | The most colleagues they cover on any one day. |

## Reading it

A high **At Once** means someone covers several people at the same time, on top of their own work: that is where things get dropped. Spread the cover on the next handovers.
