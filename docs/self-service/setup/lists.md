**You will have the list of separation types an Employee Separation picks from.**

**Who:** System Manager (other roles only where your site grants them) · **Before you start:** nothing.

## Steps

1. Type **Employee Separation Type** in the search bar and open the list.
2. Click **+ Add Employee Separation Type**.
3. Enter the **Separation Type**, such as "Resignation", "Retirement" or "End of contract".
4. Click **Save**.

## Employee separation types

Each type has only a name. An **Employee Separation** (Frappe HR's record of someone leaving) must name one.

## What happens next

The new type is offered in **Separation Type** on the Employee Separation form.
