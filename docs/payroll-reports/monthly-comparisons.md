Two reports compare a month's payroll with the month before, so you can explain every change before you pay.

Both take **From** and **To** (required), **Company** (required), **Currency**, **Employee**, **Department** and **Document Status** (**Submitted** by default). The month compared is the month of **From**; it is set against the month before it.

| Report | What it shows |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Salary Register Summary With Monthly Comparison** | By **Department** and **Salary Component**: the number of employees, gross pay and each component, for both months, with the **Difference Amount** |
| **Employee Salary Register With Monthly Comparison** | One row per employee: gross pay for each month and the **Gross Difference Amount** |
