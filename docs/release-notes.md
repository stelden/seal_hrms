What changed for users in recent releases, newest first.

## 1.22.0 (November 2026)

- A user guide: **SEAL HRMS User Guide** in the desk's Help menu, and a link to the right page in the Help menu of each form and report SEAL HRMS adds or changes.

## 1.21.0 (November 2026)

- Shorter, clearer field names and descriptions across time off claims, the Working Time Policy, handovers, the handover policy, statutory numbers on the employee record and timesheet approval. The Self Service sidebar now shows **TOIL Balances** and **Timesheet Cross-Check**.

## 1.20.0 (November 2026)

- Time off claims move by a workflow: send, authorise, approve, return and reject are in the claim's **Actions** menu, and **Return** or **Reject** ask for the reason first. HR can change who takes each step.
- Where your site runs its own workflow on timesheets, that workflow approves them.

## 1.19.0 to 1.19.4 (November 2026)

- **Claim as Time Off in Lieu** on an approved timesheet with extra hours.
- New Working Time Policies start with sensible values: 1.5 for an extra hour on a working day, 2 on a rest day or public holiday, at least 1 hour, usable for 90 days, claims within 30 days, at most 10 days held.
- My HR greets you by name, with tiles for time off in lieu and timesheets.
- Time off claim lines no longer ask for a project and task.

## 1.18.0 (November 2026)

- The weekly check on timesheets, and the **Timesheets Against Attendance and Leave** report for HR.

## 1.17.0 (November 2026)

- Timesheets by the week, in a grid, with **Copy last week**.

## 1.16.0 (November 2026)

- Timesheet approval, where a company asks for it. Staff opening a new timesheet no longer meet a permission error.

## 1.15.0 (November 2026)

- **Authorise first, then confirm the hours** for time off claims, emails at each step, and the claim's process map.

## 1.14.0 (November 2026)

- Time off claims show what attendance, clock-ins and timesheets record beside each day, with **Fill from Records**, and **Apply for Leave** once approved.

## 1.12.0 and 1.13.0 (November 2026)

- Time off in lieu: claim extra hours, have them approved and take them as leave. Unused time lapses claim by claim, and the **Time Off in Lieu** report shows each person's balance.
